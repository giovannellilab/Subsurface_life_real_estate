Subsurface Life Real Estate

A microbial cell dimensions × pore and fracture geometry × subsurface habitability framework.

Living Research Report / Work in progress. Analyses and interpretations may change as data coverage, source verification, and the scientific framework develop.

Current scientific question

What limits do microbial cell dimensions and morphology place on physical access to pore throats, connected pore networks, and fracture apertures in the subsurface? The project is assembling the evidence needed to answer that question without assuming that a database-reported dimension already represents a transit-relevant cell geometry.

Current state of evidence

Milestone 1 establishes a reproducible, source-preserving census of BacDive type-strain morphology records and an offline LPSN GSS cross-check. It reports source dimensions, nomenclature evidence, missingness, and quality-control signals. It does not yet estimate cell volume or diameter, model pore accessibility, or claim a representative distribution for all prokaryotes.

Scientific quality control

LPSN GSS supports 21,201 strain records with name-and-type evidence; 83 are name-supported with unresolved type confirmation, 217 are status-review cases, 597 names are unmatched, and 24 have ambiguous exact matches. BacDive/LPSN disagreements are retained as review signals: 1,188 embedded-name discrepancies, 4,961 deposits linked to other LPSN taxa, and 84 no-overlap type-deposit cases.

Scientific QC v1 reviewed 1,288 suspect observations. Two have high-confidence, source-verified unit overlays; 1,286 remain unresolved. Raw and normalized source values remain immutable, and overlays do not replace them.

Current interpretation and claim boundaries

Under the stated selection rules, the canonical species-level minimum-width median is 0.55 µm for 4,452 strictly LPSN-supported species. The 1 µm value lies near the upper part of this source-conditioned canonical distribution. This supports a limited descriptive observation only. It does not establish a universal microbial width, a pore-accessibility limit, fracture transit, or the habitability of any subsurface setting.

Coverage varies across source taxonomic labels and remains a database-coverage association after taxonomy harmonization. Complex morphologies, source ambiguity, multiple observations, and unresolved scale anomalies prevent direct geometric interpretation.

Unresolved questions and next analytical steps

  • Define defensible geometry conventions for rods, cocci, filaments, spores, aggregates, and pleomorphic cells.
  • Complete primary-source review for unresolved unit and scale anomalies.
  • Establish a scientific policy for taxonomic reconciliation and dependence among observations.
  • Specify pore-throat, network-connectivity, and fracture-aperture models before inferring physical accessibility.
  • Quantify how source coverage and taxonomic representation constrain extrapolation to subsurface communities.
