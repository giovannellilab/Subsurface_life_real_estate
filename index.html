Framework

Overview

Current scientific question

What limits do microbial cell dimensions and morphology place on physical access to pore throats, connected pore networks, and fracture apertures in the subsurface? The project is assembling the evidence needed to answer that question without assuming that a database-reported dimension already represents a transit-relevant cell geometry.

Conceptual framework

The framework will bring together microbial dimensions and morphology, the size and connectedness of pore space, and fracture apertures. Each component must retain its own evidence and uncertainty before it can support an accessibility inference.

Current state of evidence

Milestone 1 establishes a reproducible, source-preserving census of BacDive type-strain morphology records and an offline LPSN GSS cross-check. It reports source dimensions, nomenclature evidence, missingness, and quality-control signals. It does not yet estimate cell volume or diameter, model pore accessibility, or claim a representative distribution for all prokaryotes.

Current interpretation

Under the stated selection rules, the canonical species-level minimum-width median is 0.55 µm for 4,452 strictly LPSN-supported species. The 1 µm value lies near the upper part of this source-conditioned canonical distribution. This supports a limited descriptive observation only. It does not establish a universal microbial width, a pore-accessibility limit, fracture transit, or the habitability of any subsurface setting.

Morphology and dimension evidence

The analysis-eligible observation subset excludes provisional complex, conflicting, and ambiguous morphology contexts for descriptive summaries only. It remains observation-level: repeated reports are neither averaged nor made into a single species representative.

Microbial-dataset scientific QC

LPSN GSS supports 21,201 strain records with name-and-type evidence; 83 are name-supported with unresolved type confirmation, 217 are status-review cases, 597 names are unmatched, and 24 have ambiguous exact matches. BacDive/LPSN disagreements are retained as review signals: 1,188 embedded-name discrepancies, 4,961 deposits linked to other LPSN taxa, and 84 no-overlap type-deposit cases.

Scientific QC v1 reviewed 1,288 suspect observations. Two have high-confidence, source-verified unit overlays; 1,286 remain unresolved. Raw and normalized source values remain immutable, and overlays do not replace them.

Empirical component 2

Pore & fracture space

The intended geological component will compile evidence about pore-throat size, pore connectivity, and fracture aperture in relevant subsurface settings. Its purpose is to characterize the physical pathways against which microbial dimensions may eventually be evaluated.

Under development. An empirical geological compilation has not yet been completed. This report therefore presents no pore-throat, connectivity, or fracture-aperture results.

Integration

Accessibility framework

The planned analysis will integrate geometry-relevant microbial evidence with pore-throat size, connected pore networks, and fracture aperture. It will need explicit conventions for which cell dimension is relevant to each morphology and physical pathway.

Planned analysis, not current evidence. No pore-accessibility or transit model has been calculated in Milestone 1. Future integration must distinguish source-reported dimensions, verified analytical overlays, morphology-specific geometry choices, and geological constraints.

Boundaries

Limitations & open questions

Coverage varies across source taxonomic labels and remains a database-coverage association after taxonomy harmonization. Complex morphologies, source ambiguity, multiple observations, and unresolved scale anomalies prevent direct geometric interpretation. The current census must not be described as representative of all prokaryotic diversity.

  • Define defensible geometry conventions for rods, cocci, filaments, spores, aggregates, and pleomorphic cells.
  • Complete primary-source review for unresolved unit and scale anomalies.
  • Establish a scientific policy for taxonomic reconciliation and dependence among observations.
  • Specify pore-throat, network-connectivity, and fracture-aperture models before inferring physical accessibility.
  • Quantify how source coverage and taxonomic representation constrain extrapolation to subsurface communities.

Traceability

Methods & provenance

Milestone 1 uses documented BacDive searches, an immutable local LPSN GSS CSV, source-preserving normalization, and versioned QC overlays. The analysis retains source values and labels, explicit missingness, cross-database discrepancies, and unresolved observations rather than silently correcting or averaging them.
