A. Scientific results

Where measured geological geometry meets cultured microbial width

The current proof of concept identifies microbial-scale overlap in Lipnice granite MIP entry equivalents and the W23 modelled shale pore-cluster curve. Most currently available CT/PNM networks are larger than cultured microbial widths within their resolved observational windows.

Pore × microbe proof of concept

For comparison only, an explicit geological comparison diameter is derived as 2 × radius when a source explicitly reports a radius. Source diameters and Lipnice MIP entry/throat-equivalent dimensions are retained. The result separates accommodation in a pore body or modelled pore cluster from nominal transit through a throat or entry constriction. Connectivity and transport are reported separately.

Granite’s entry-equivalent distribution and W23’s modelled pore-cluster distribution visibly overlap the microbial-width domain. In contrast, the currently measured CT/PNM body and throat distributions mostly lie at larger reported diameters. That is a result about the reported measurement windows, not an estimate of whole-rock accessible space.

Resolution matters. The high reported throat overlaps for Fontainebleau/Berea (0.953 at midpoint width) and F42A sand (0.999) are conditional on their 0.74 µm and 9.996 µm CT/PNM windows, respectively. Basalt accommodation is conditional on 14.99 µm CT resolution. Carbonate and Wilmslow local artifacts do not state a voxel size, so that absence remains explicit. None of these values is accessibility, habitability, colonizable porosity, or connected habitat.

The existing clearance curves remain informative only for the separately justified granite MIP and dry Fontainebleau/Berea throat comparison. They show large Lipnice specimen variation and are retained below as technical material.

C. Methods & technical provenance

What the measurements mean

Native geological values remain source-preserving. The microbial comparison uses a separate analytical comparison diameter only where semantics permit it: explicit radius → 2 × radius; source diameter retained; or documented MIP entry/throat-equivalent dimension retained. Unresolved Harvard CTSTA classes and connectivity-only records receive no comparison diameter.

Current measurement roles and observational windows
RoleExamplesWeighting / window
AccommodationPNM pore bodies; W23 modelled pore clusterObject count or modelled volume; CT/PNM resolution and segmentation remain source-specific
Nominal transitLipnice MIP entry equivalents; PNM throatsIncremental intruded porosity or throat-object count; never pooled across methods
Connectivity contextCoordination, topology, porosity, permeability, resistivity, formation factorReported separately; not included in geometric compatibility

F42A retains separate pore nodes, throat edges, coordination, volumes, and source transport metrics in a 9.996 µm resolved network. PANGAEA Atlantis Massif serpentinised-ultramafic and mafic records contribute bulk porosity and pressure-dependent transport only, not a size distribution. Raw third-party artifacts, interim extraction, complete processed tables, and acquisition caches remain local and gitignored.

Framework & next questions

What this does—and does not—establish

The project extends pore-size thinking by using empirical microbial widths, distinguishing bodies from constrictions, and retaining measurement meaning. The present result is local geometry. A material can have pore space that accommodates a cell but lacks a cell-transit path; it can also have hydraulic or solute connectivity without cellular connectivity.

The cultured baseline is conditional on cultivation, formal description, and morphology recording. It is not yet an environmental or subsurface microbial-size distribution. Fracture aperture and connectivity remain a separate future resource.

Next scientific questions

  • How does the cultured baseline compare with directly observed environmental and subsurface cell dimensions?
  • Which constriction metric is most biologically relevant across MIP, CT/PNM, microscopy, and fractures?
  • When can connectivity, orientation, deformability, and biofilms be added without collapsing distinct mechanisms into one metric?

Scientific basis
